# Priora — Real work. Explicit decisions.
## A detailed storyboard for a 90-second motion film

**Format:** 1920 × 1080, 30 frames per second, 90 seconds.  
**Character:** A moving illustration on warm paper. Precise, tactile and quietly expressive.  
**Narration:** A calm British voice, conversational and unhurried.  
**Story:** A worker describes a repair. Priora assembles the relevant agents, checks the conditions and brings a deviation to the person responsible. Three decision rooms explore what can be retained, mitigated or transferred. The human makes the decision.

The film presents a proposed system. Transfer is explicitly simulated.

---

## The visual world

The entire film lives on one large sheet of warm, off-white paper. Its surface has a faint, uneven grain, visible in the quieter areas. The paper stays still beneath the drawing; it does not flicker or crawl.

Everything feels printed, cut and carefully arranged. The coloured shapes have the slight irregularity of ink pressed into paper: cobalt with tiny cream flecks, coral with softer variations in density, charcoal with a fine worn texture. Edges remain clean enough to read at a distance.

The camera travels across this single composition. It approaches a detail, follows an object along a thread, then withdraws to reveal how that detail belongs to the larger system. There are no cuts. Camera moves ease into moments of stillness so the viewer can look.

The composition has three territories:

- **Below the centre:** the real world—a worker, an industrial site and a risk owner.
- **To the left:** the configurable site panel, arranged around an open circular table.
- **To the right:** three rounded decision chambers, joined by a small shared forecourt.

A fine record line runs underneath the real world. It acquires small marks as checks and decisions are recorded.

Large areas of paper remain empty. At any moment, one arrangement receives the viewer’s attention. Other parts of the system remain faintly visible at the edges or sit beyond the frame.

### Colour and meaning

**Black** belongs to the physical world, ordinary language and human authority. The worker, factory and risk owner are black. The line that authorises a decision is always black.

**Cobalt blue** is the principal colour of Priora and its agents. It supplies the strongest visual presence: confident, distinct and deeply printed.

**Muted coral** highlights questions, exceptions and selected agent details. It brings warmth to the system without acting as a flashing alarm.

**Stone and pale cream** mark unoccupied places, supporting structures and inactive possibilities.

Colour supports identity, but never carries meaning alone. Shapes and short labels distinguish roles. A dashed outline indicates something proposed, incomplete or simulated.

### The cast of agents

The agents are small abstract glyphs with individual silhouettes. They are expressive through orientation and movement, without faces or limbs.

**Priora** is a cobalt ring with a generous paper-coloured opening. A fine outer orbit and one small cobalt bead distinguish it from every other agent. The bead moves towards whatever Priora is attending to.

The specialist agents form a related family:

- **Site rules:** two stacked semicircular forms, suggesting a measured, balanced structure.
- **Insurer conditions:** two opposing bracket-like pieces with a narrow opening between them.
- **Fire:** a circular form with a clean wedge removed.
- **Risk engineering:** an arch with a semicircular opening.
- **Evidence check:** a softened square with a small circular aperture.

Their geometry comes from the same vocabulary of circles, cuts, openings and paired pieces. Each retains its silhouette throughout the film.

Movement gives them character. Site rules aligns precisely. Insurer conditions closes around a detail. Fire turns towards the relevant activity. Risk engineering opens out to examine its surroundings. Evidence check frames and settles over an image.

Priora moves with a slightly longer, smoother glide. Its orbiting bead anticipates the direction of travel before the main ring follows.

### The case

The case is a separate object that Priora carries.

At first, it is a thin ring around a small black centre: the actual job. Six small facets gather around it, each holding a fact. As the checks develop, coloured arcs assemble around this core.

When a condition is unmet, a gap appears in the surrounding ring. That gap remains visible as the case moves through the decision rooms. It is the film’s recurring image of unresolved risk.

The case never changes into an unrelated symbol. The viewer can recognise it from the worker’s first message to the final recorded decision.

### Typography and sound

Names and short statements use IBM Plex Sans. Small status labels use IBM Plex Mono with restrained letter spacing. The typography is dark, crisp and spacious.

The sound world is intimate: a soft paper contact, a dry wooden tick, a fine plucked thread, a muted felt landing. A low, warm musical bed develops gradually. There are no loud alerts or dramatic rises.

The worker’s message sounds like a real phone recording. The narrator sounds close and composed.

## Sequence 1 — Real work in the middle
**0:00–0:08 · 240 frames · F0000–0239**

We begin on empty paper.

A thin black line draws slowly from left to right across the lower portion of the frame. Its leading edge has a tiny suggestion of ink arriving on the surface.

Above it, a short sentence appears:

**Real work in the middle.**

Three black silhouettes arrive on the line.

First, a worker wearing a hard hat. The figure is simple and solid, with a slightly rounded stance and one arm resting near a phone.

Second, an industrial building: a low roofline, one taller service block and a small rectangular window. The building is spare enough to read instantly, with no decorative detail.

Third, the risk owner, seated behind a desk. A horizontal desktop and a curved shoulder distinguish this person from the worker.

The figures settle with quiet, almost imperceptible contact shadows. Small labels appear underneath:

**WORKER**  
**SITE**  
**RISK OWNER**

The narrator names each as it arrives.

On the word “Priora”, a cobalt ring appears above the group. Its small satellite bead moves through a short arc, as though looking across the three elements.

A fine elliptical path grows from the ring and loosely surrounds the real world. Five specialist glyphs appear at intervals along it. They arrive with different small turns, then settle into a balanced arrangement.

A second sentence appears beneath the first, this time in cobalt:

**Agents around it.**

For a moment, the whole relationship is visible: physical work below, the agents surrounding it, the human clearly present.

The ellipse then withdraws. The specialist glyphs drift towards the edge of the composition and soften out of view. Priora remains and glides towards the worker.

**Narration**

“Real work in the middle: a worker, a site, a risk owner. Priora places agents around it.”

**Motion and sound**

The first eight seconds establish restraint. Nothing bounces. The three silhouettes arrive with soft print-like contacts; the agents introduce a few delicate, higher notes.

## Sequence 2 — A voice becomes a case
**0:08–0:22 · 420 frames · F0240–0659**

The camera moves closer to the worker, leaving part of the factory visible to the right.

The worker raises the phone. A small black waveform begins to grow horizontally from it. Words appear above the waveform in short spoken groups.

**Worker’s voice**

“Hey, the bracket by the packing line has cracked again. We’re going to weld it before the night shift.”

The message is the busiest moment of ordinary language in the film. It feels human and specific.

Priora hovers above and to the right. Its satellite bead turns towards the waveform. A fine dotted cobalt thread extends from the bead to the growing end of the message.

Tiny cobalt dots travel back along this thread. They move in small groups, suggesting that Priora is listening as the message unfolds.

Selected words receive short printed underlines:

**bracket**  
**cracked**  
**packing line**  
**weld**  
**before the night shift**

The underlines use cobalt, with a single coral accent beneath “cracked”.

As the message ends, the remaining words fade into pale grey. The important phrases stay dark and separate gently from the sentence.

The camera rises with them.

Priora draws a thin circular outline. At its centre sits a small black dot: the real job. The selected phrases move into positions around this core.

They simplify into four small facet chips:

**REPAIR**  
**HOT WORK**  
**PACKING LINE**  
**BEFORE NIGHT SHIFT**

Each chip combines a simple mark with its label: a bracket shape, a small spark, a location corner, a clock arc.

A line rises from the factory and contributes a fifth facet:

**SITE + INSURANCE CONDITIONS**

A sixth position remains empty. It is a dashed circle with a small question mark and the label:

**PHOTO**

Priora’s bead rotates towards the worker. A dotted thread descends, carrying the short request:

**Photo of the bracket?**

The worker tilts the phone. A little rectangular image travels upwards: a monochrome illustration of an L-shaped bracket with a clear crack at its bend.

The image settles into the empty facet. Its dashed perimeter becomes continuous.

The six facets fold slightly closer to the centre. The arrangement becomes compact enough to travel while remaining recognisable.

Beneath it, one word appears:

**CASE**

**Narration, following the worker’s message**

“Priora hears the job, and asks for a photo.”

**Motion and sound**

The words move like pieces of printed paper lifted and placed elsewhere. The photo lands with a soft card contact. A restrained musical pattern begins beneath the assembled case.

## Sequence 3 — The site chooses its cast
**0:22–0:38 · 480 frames · F0660–1139**

Priora travels left with the case following just behind it on a short thread. The camera accompanies them.

The site panel enters the frame as a large circular enclosure seen from above. Its boundary is a fine stone-coloured line, interrupted by a generous entrance on the right.

A pale circular table sits inside. Eight small positions are marked around it. The architecture is flat and graphic, with the spatial clarity of an overhead plan.

Above the enclosure:

**Site panel**

Below that, in small mono type:

**CONFIGURED FOR THIS SITE**

Three positions contain barely visible ghost glyphs labelled Electrical, Structural and Security. They establish that the panel could contain other specialists.

Priora’s bead turns briefly towards those seats, then returns to the case. The ghosts remain pale and inactive.

The camera holds while the relevant specialists arrive.

First, the photo facet pulses once. A cobalt thread reaches outward, and **Evidence check** slides into view. Its rounded square turns a quarter rotation, straightens and passes through the entrance.

Next, the packing-line facet responds. **Risk engineering** arrives, its arch shape opening towards the case.

The site conditions summon **Site rules**, whose paired semicircles align as it settles.

The hot-work facet summons **Fire**, a notched disc that rotates until its opening faces the case.

Finally, **Insurer conditions** enters, its two bracket pieces travelling together with a small, precise gap between them.

Each agent uses the same entrance. Their routes curve naturally around one another. Faint, short-lived echoes follow the moving glyphs, suggesting direction without filling the drawing with trails.

The case passes through the opening and settles at the centre of the table.

Priora stays outside.

Its bead faces inward, and a fine connection remains between Priora and the case. This arrangement holds long enough to make its role clear: Priora assembles and conducts the panel.

Inside, the case opens into its facets.

The checks happen as a short sequence of distinct gestures:

- Evidence check frames the bracket image; its small aperture centres on the crack.
- Risk engineering draws a fine dashed radius around the packing-line facet.
- Site rules places a short horizontal mark beside the job.
- Fire turns towards the hot-work symbol and sends a small pulse along its edge.
- Insurer conditions closes its two brackets around the conditions facet.

A small label moves between Fire and Insurer conditions:

**FIRE WATCH?**

Their answer joins the other findings.

Five small marks leave the panel through the entrance and collect beside Priora. No celebratory animation follows; the system has simply finished a piece of work.

**Narration**

“Next, it summons only the specialists this site and job need. Each checks its own conditions and reports back.”

**Motion and sound**

The agents arrive with subtly different sound signatures from the same family of wood, felt and paper. The camera remains mostly still during their checks so their individual behaviour is legible.

## Sequence 4 — When the conditions hold
**0:38–0:47 · 270 frames · F1140–1409**

The camera approaches the case at the centre of the panel.

The five specialist findings become curved segments around it. Each arc arrives from the direction of its agent.

The arcs align into one continuous cobalt ring.

A short label appears:

**INSIDE THE CONDITIONS**

This sequence shows the ordinary path when the checks are satisfied.

From the completed ring, a fine cobalt route draws through the panel entrance and across the paper towards the factory.

The camera follows it, widening until the worker, site and risk owner are visible again.

The route reaches the small factory window. A restrained white welding spark begins to flicker inside the black building. It is tiny but alive.

Below the ground line, a thin record line extends and receives one short vertical mark.

**RECORD KEPT**

Beside the risk owner:

**NO ONE DISTURBED**

The risk owner does not move. No message travels to the desk. The absence of interruption is the point of the composition.

Priora pauses between the site and the panel. Its bead makes a small attentive turn towards the continuing work.

**Narration**

“When everything holds, the route opens. Work goes on, the record is kept, no one is disturbed.”

**Motion and sound**

The closed ring resolves with a soft, clear tone. The welding sound remains faint and local. The music briefly opens into a warmer sustained chord.

## Sequence 5 — A condition slips
**0:47–0:57 · 300 frames · F1410–1709**

The camera returns towards the panel, following the route backwards.

The case is still present, but one surrounding arc loosens. It rotates slightly away from the others and becomes dashed.

The Insurer conditions glyph separates its two pieces by a few millimetres.

A small gap now interrupts the ring.

Coral appears at the two exposed ends of the gap. The accent is measured and quiet.

Beside the case, two horizontal rulers draw one above the other:

**FIRE WATCH PLANNED: 30 MIN**

A solid line extends to its endpoint.

**POLICY ASKS: 60 MIN**

A dashed line extends twice as far.

The comparison holds plainly on screen. The viewer can understand the difference without the narrator.

Near the route to the factory, a pale barrier outline appears. It remains incomplete and is labelled:

**NO HARD STOP CONFIGURED**

The route stays open. In the distance, the factory’s small welding spark continues.

Priora gathers the findings. The case’s facets fold inward and the surrounding ring contracts, retaining its visible gap.

The label changes to:

**DECISION PACKET**

Priora carries it out through the panel entrance and across the sheet towards the risk owner.

The camera follows.

At the desk, the packet stops. A thicker black line extends from the risk owner, curves around it and forms a loose enclosing loop.

This line has greater visual weight than the fine agent threads. Its origin at the person’s desk is unmistakable.

A label appears alongside it:

**RISK OWNER DECIDES**

**Narration**

“One condition slips: the fire watch is half the policy. Priora brings it to the risk owner. Agents prepare, the human decides.”

**Motion and sound**

The separating arc produces a dry, soft tick. The music leaves a small unresolved space. When the human line arrives, a low wooden note adds weight without implying that a decision has already been made.

## Sequence 6 — Three decision rooms
**0:57–1:18 · 630 frames · F1710–2339**

The camera moves right and slightly upwards.

Three circular chambers appear in a clover-like arrangement around a small shared forecourt. Fine paths connect that forecourt to the risk owner.

Each room has a clear opening facing the shared space. The walls are drawn as broad, flat bands of printed colour, with the paper visible inside.

**Retain** has a muted coral band.  
**Mitigate** has a cobalt band.  
**Transfer** has a pale stone band with a dashed cobalt perimeter.

Their names appear in full, with large, spare letters beneath:

**R · M · T**

Retain and Mitigate carry the small status:

**DESIGN PROPOSAL**

Transfer carries:

**SIMULATED**

The packet remains the same object, its gap still visible.

### Retain — a deliberate acceptance
**0:57–1:04 · 210 frames**

The camera approaches Retain.

Priora brings the packet to the entrance. A fine line across the threshold remains closed.

Priora stops short. Its bead turns towards the doorway, then towards the risk owner.

A short statement appears:

**AN AGENT CANNOT CHOOSE IT**

Beside the doorway, a small grey clock arc completes a turn. The threshold remains closed.

**NO ANSWER DOES NOT CHOOSE IT**

Then the black line from the risk owner reaches the entrance. It opens the threshold and holds the passage clear.

A small label identifies the source:

**RISK OWNER**

Priora carries the packet inside.

Small room agents gather around it. A coral bar is positioned across the gap, like a bridge. The original gap remains readable beneath it: the exposure has been knowingly accepted.

Four short leader lines extend from the packet:

**EXPOSURE**  
**AUTHORITY**  
**CONDITIONS**  
**EXPIRY**

The arrangement holds as a concise picture of an explicit retained risk.

**Narration**

“Retain is never a default. Risk is kept on purpose, with its terms explicit.”

### Mitigate — compare ways to close the gap
**1:04–1:11 · 210 frames**

The camera glides across the shared forecourt towards Mitigate. The retained-risk treatment falls away, returning the packet to its unresolved state. These are alternative paths being explained.

The owner’s black line opens the Mitigate threshold.

Inside, three safeguard glyphs rest in a neat horizontal arrangement. Their labels read:

**Thermal check**  
**Extend watch to 60 min**  
**Move weld to workshop**

Above them:

**SUGGESTIONS FROM A REVIEWED LIBRARY**

Each proposal has two small visual measures: a stack of discs for cost and an open clock arc for time. These indicate comparison without inventing numerical estimates.

As Priora attends to each option, it rises slightly from the paper and moves towards the packet.

The thermal-check proposal appears as a dashed piece that fills only part of the gap.

**PARTIAL**

It withdraws.

The longer-watch proposal extends across the full opening. The ring becomes continuous in preview.

**FULL**

Evidence check’s corner marks settle around the result.

**BACK INSIDE**

The full ring is held briefly, then the proposal withdraws and returns to its place. The packet is unresolved again; this demonstration has not applied a safeguard.

**Narration**

“Mitigate compares safeguards, and checks whether the work is back inside.”

### Transfer — a simulated inquiry
**1:11–1:18 · 210 frames**

The camera continues to Transfer.

Its dashed boundary is immediately apparent. Two persistent labels sit above it:

**SIMULATED**  
**NO INSURER ON PRIORA YET**

The human line authorises the inquiry at the threshold. Priora carries the packet inside.

Three small dashed agent glyphs sit around the perimeter, with short labels identifying simulated carrier and capacity roles. They are drawn with less visual density than the established site agents.

Dashed copies of the unresolved gap travel out from the packet along fine paths. They pass through small openings in the chamber boundary and reach the simulated agents.

Answers return as hollow outlined pieces.

Four labels collect beside the packet:

**ELIGIBILITY**  
**TERMS**  
**SAFEGUARDS**  
**PRICE ?**

There is no insurer logo, numerical quote or indication that cover has been placed.

The answers remain dashed.

Priora gathers them and returns the packet towards the risk owner.

**Narration**

“Transfer, simulated for now, asks outside capacity for terms and a price.”

**Motion and sound across the rooms**

The rooms share one sound palette. Retain has a slightly lower contact sound; Mitigate uses two light comparison notes; Transfer has a thinner, more open tone. The transitions are camera journeys through the shared drawing.

## Sequence 7 — The rooms work together
**1:18–1:24 · 180 frames · F2340–2519**

The camera widens to include all three chambers and the risk owner’s desk.

The packet travels through a compact sequence of possible combinations. The routes are already visible, so the viewer follows the same object through familiar space.

At Mitigate, a dashed safeguard fills part of the gap.

A small label joins the packet:

**MITIGATE PART**

At Transfer, a simulated inquiry returns a hollow question mark.

**WHAT WOULD IT COST?**

At Retain, a proposed coral bridge spans the remaining opening.

**KEEP THE REST**

At each threshold, the risk owner’s black line reaches the doorway before Priora crosses it. The gestures are economical and clear.

Priora returns to the desk with the packet and a short list of the explored options.

The risk owner’s black line closes around the chosen combination.

The selected mitigation and retention marks become solid. The simulated transfer inquiry remains dashed and off to one side, preserving its status as an explored possibility.

One soft pulse passes through the human line.

**RISK OWNER DECIDES**

**Narration**

“Priora carries the case between rooms. The risk owner stays in control.”

**Motion and sound**

The travel is quicker here, but the final arrival pauses. The unresolved musical interval settles only when the human line closes.

## Sequence 8 — The whole system
**1:24–1:30 · 180 frames · F2520–2699**

The camera withdraws smoothly to reveal the complete sheet.

The site panel sits to the left, with its selected specialist glyphs around the table and a few empty positions.

The worker, factory and risk owner occupy the lower centre.

The three decision chambers sit to the right.

Fine paths connect them. Their curves are balanced and spacious, creating the sense of one coherent system rather than a dense network.

The decided case lowers gently towards the record line. It leaves a second small mark.

The factory window still flickers.

Priora rises to the upper centre of the composition. Its satellite bead settles between the site panel and the decision rooms.

A restrained label appears above the drawing:

**THE COMPLETE SYSTEM IS A DESIGN PROPOSAL**

Three short statements sit beside the structures they describe:

**ONE PRIORA AGENT**  
**A CONFIGURABLE SITE PANEL**  
**THREE DECISION ROOMS**

At the lower edge, a smaller line reads:

**FIRST: HOT WORK · POSSIBLY LATER: OTHER INSURED ACTIVITIES**

One pale, dashed specialist glyph approaches an empty panel position, suggesting that another activity could require another configuration. It stays visibly provisional.

The drawing then recedes in contrast. The people, rooms and routes remain faintly visible through the paper-like atmosphere.

The final statement appears large and dark:

**Priora turns physical work  
into explicit risk decisions,  
while the work happens.**

The small welding spark remains visible for one last moment beneath the words.

Over the final second and a half, the surrounding drawing and statement dissolve, leaving only:

**Priora**

Black type, centred on warm paper.

**Narration**

“Priora turns physical work into explicit risk decisions, while the work happens.”

**Sound**

The music resolves softly. A faint paper contact accompanies the final record mark. The last moment is nearly silent.

## The feeling throughout

The agents should feel like a recognisable cast of thoughtfully shaped objects. A turn towards the case communicates attention. A measured alignment communicates checking. A passage through a doorway communicates entering a different decision process.

The texture supplies warmth; the geometry supplies clarity.

The continuous camera makes the relationships tangible. Every close-up belongs to a larger place the viewer has already begun to understand. By the final pullback, the whole system feels familiar.

The story’s visual anchors remain consistent: the black physical world, Priora’s cobalt ring, the travelling case, its unresolved gap and the human decision line. Together, they let the film explain a complicated mechanism with relatively few words.

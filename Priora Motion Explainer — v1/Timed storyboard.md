# Priora motion explainer v1: timed storyboard

90.0 seconds, 1920 x 1080, 30 fps. Times are film seconds, as built. A word in brackets is the voice word the picture lands on, with its time. On-screen words are quoted exactly as they appear: CAPITALS where they are set in small mono capitals, sentence case where they are set in Plex Sans.

The world is one sheet of warm paper. The real world (worker, site, risk owner) is black ink in the middle at the bottom. Priora and its specialists are rust die-cut glyphs. The site panel sits to the left, the three decision rooms to the right. Black is always the human; rust is always the agents. One camera moves between still frames.

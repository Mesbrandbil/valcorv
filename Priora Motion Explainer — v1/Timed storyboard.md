# Priora motion explainer v1: timed storyboard

90.0 seconds, 1920 x 1080, 30 fps. Times are film seconds. Word times in brackets are when the voice says that word; the picture lands on them. On-screen text is shown in CAPITALS when it is set in small mono capitals, and in quotes when it is set in sentence case.

The world is one sheet of warm paper. The real world (worker, site, risk owner) is black ink in the middle at the bottom. Priora and its specialists are rust die-cut glyphs. The site panel sits to the left, the three decision rooms to the right. One camera moves between still frames; it never moves while something has to be read.

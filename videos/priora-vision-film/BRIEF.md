## Intent

A premium, website-embeddable vision film for Priora, the product made by Valcorv. It must make one idea understandable to a normal intelligent viewer: the gap between live physical work and the conditions under which risk was accepted is usually found afterwards; Priora connects them live, so the decision exists when the risk changes. Prevention and proof today; over time, record becomes trust, decision, price and capacity.

Feel: Silicon Valley launch-film production quality with Apple-like restraint. Architectural working drawing and forensic reconstruction, never a whiteboard explainer. Quiet confidence, a decisive rewind, sudden clarity, a calm inevitable close.

The full production prompt is the governing brief: `docs/production-prompt.md` (verbatim copy of the user's upload).

## Assets

- ../../priora-end-state-demo.html — the Priora end-state interface. The film's destination and the source of the Nordhavn site geometry, envelope, node glyphs, activity card, decision sheet, record and chain. Read-only: never edited; film adaptations live in this project.
- assets/brand/priora-wordmark.svg — type-led Priora wordmark (IBM Plex Sans SemiBold), from the Valcorv Drive. Only the wordmark is used from that folder; the older brand manual it sits with describes a retired positioning and does not govern this film.

## Customizations

- HyperFrames is the production framework (user's explicit choice).
- ElevenLabs narration with word or character timing driving the edit. A local guide voice is used only to lock the build while ElevenLabs is unreachable from this environment; the final film must be re-locked to the ElevenLabs performance.
- Signature rewind: picture and sound reverse to the instant before Sprinkler Zone 3 goes offline.
- Cobalt blue signal colour replaces the demo's orange in the film adaptation only.
- Deliverables: H.264 MP4, WebM, source project, narration script and audio, timing data, master and stems (voice, music, SFX), storyboard, contact sheet.

## Notes

- Priora is the product, Valcorv is the company. No retired product names.
- Claims: prevention and proof today. Speech intake, live condition mapping, automatic deviation recognition, retain and transfer flows, carrier quotes, pricing and capacity are conceptual or future state. Never "uninsured"; say the insurance position may have changed. Priora never prices, ranks carriers or replaces the annual programme.
- No future calendar dates on screen (the demo's renewal date is dropped).
- Nordhavn Bioprocessing, all people, carriers, quotes and prices are fictional.
- Exclusions: stock footage, glossy 3D, cartoon people, whiteboard hands, neon, purple gradients, glassmorphism, card-grid SaaS, flames, disaster spectacle, handshakes, shields, locks, floating documents.
- Rendering was authorised in the brief itself ("carry the project through ... rendering, and verification").
